## Description

Smart Expense AI helps individuals and small teams keep a clear view of shared income and expenses. Create a workspace, record income and spending, organise categories, upload receipts or invoices, and review reports for the selected period.

- See backend-confirmed income, expenses, and remaining balance.
- Add, edit, filter, and organise records according to your workspace role.
- Capture a receipt with the device camera or select an image or PDF to upload.
- Keep receipts private and attached to the relevant record.
- Review optional AI extraction suggestions before confirming them; nothing affects totals until confirmed.
- Switch between personal or team workspaces while keeping data isolated.
- Use English (LTR) or Arabic (RTL).

Smart Expense AI is free to download and use. It has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, trials, payment features, or paywalls. It requires an account and internet connection for account and financial data operations. It does not offer offline writes or queued financial changes.
